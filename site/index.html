What is real

Neurons, synapses and their signs come from the Janelia / Google male CNS v1.0 connectome; the neuron model follows Shiu et al., Nature 2024. How a drink or a bike's lean maps onto sensory neurons is modeled, and so is the bike decoder.

Where it ran

On a Slurm node with RTX A6000 GPUs. One whole-brain trial takes ~8 s; batched on the GPU, 128 trials take 35 s, and 32 brains ride at ~14 s of wall time per second of bike time.
